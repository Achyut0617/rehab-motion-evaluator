    Edge computing on the Jetson: why, what breaks, and how it compares
    
    
    Why the project runs on a Jetson next to the patient, every hardware and software failure you are likely to hit, and how the Jetson, the models and the precision choices compare with the alternatives. Figures marked calc are our own arithmetic from sourced inputs. All other figures come from published sources listed at the end.
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    
      
    Latency
    ~45–80 ms on the Jetson vs ~180–260 ms via cloud calc
    India's median latency to cloud regions is 108–158 ms (Ookla, 2026) before any upload or inference. Motor-learning studies found ~60 ms less visual feedback delay raised implicit learning by ~50%, with the effect strongest under 100 ms.
    
      
    Bandwidth
    0.5–4.3 GB/hour of video vs 6–60 MB/hour of angles calc
    Sending only numbers is a 100–1000× reduction. MJPEG at 640×480 would use ~60% of India's median 5G uplink (15.75 Mbps).
    
      
    Privacy
    Video never leaves the room
    Only joint angles are stored. The LLM receives age, condition and metrics, never the name or images. This simplifies duties under India's DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025.

    Thermal limits
    
        
    
        
    Software throttling starts at 99 °C, hardware throttling at 103 °C (clocks cut 50 / 75 / 87.5%), shutdown at ~104.5–105 °C. The default fan profile is "quiet". Since L4T 32.4 plain jetson_clocks no longer forces the fan; add --fan.

    Why INT8 hurts pose more than detection: keypoint coordinates are fine-grained regression outputs. Rounding activations to 8-bit shifts each joint by a few pixels, and the angle maths magnifies those shifts.

    How accurate are camera-measured joint angles?
    
      
    
        
    StudyCompared withResult
    
        
          
    OpenPose gait (Stenum 2021)Marker-based motion captureMean error hip 4.0°, knee 5.6°, ankle 7.4° (sagittal plane)
    
          
    MediaPipe knee (JMIR 2026 preprint, not peer-reviewed)GoniometerStatic ICC 0.874, RMSE ≈15.5°; dynamic tasks underestimated ≈5°; single-leg stance ICC 0.136 (poor)
    
          
    Cycling studiesMotion capture3–9° error
    
          
    Frontal-plane knee valgusViconAbsolute angles not valid
    
          
    BlazePose elbow flexionReference systemsUnderestimates large flexion; error grows with angle, so a fixed offset cannot correct it
    
            
      
    
      
    Realistic expectation: about 4–8° error, and only with the camera side-on at joint height. That is why the README calls scores "computational indicators, not clinically validated assessments".
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Camera set-up protocol
      
        
    • Camera perpendicular to the moving limb (sagittal view)
    • Lens at the height of the joint being measured
    • 2–3 m away so hip, knee and ankle stay in frame
    • Even lighting, fitted clothing, plain background
    • Patient's tested side facing the camera
    
      
    Validate it yourself (viva-ready)
      
        
    1. Hold 5 knee angles (90°…180°) measured by a goniometer
    2. Record the app's angle for each, 3 trials
    3. Report mean absolute error and RMSE
    4. Plot Bland-Altman (difference vs mean) and compute ICC
    5. Repeat with the camera rotated 30° to show the 3D benefit
